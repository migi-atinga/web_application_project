The problem we address

Many local organizations struggle to find volunteers with the right skills and availability, while willing volunteers often do not know where their time is needed. This system gives volunteers a single, simple place to register their interests, skills, and availability so coordinators can match them to real community activities.

Who uses this system

Volunteers

Members of the public who want to offer their time and skills to community activities.

Volunteer coordinators

Staff who review submitted registrations and assign volunteers to activities.
